The challenge

The specific demands of public-sector roofs

City halls, courthouses, public safety buildings, and civic facilities aren't procured or managed like private property. The contractor has to fit the process — formal bids, compliance paperwork, public accountability — while keeping essential services running under an open roof.

  • Formal public bid and procurement processes with strict submittal requirements
  • Prevailing wage and certified payroll compliance on public works projects
  • Documentation at every step — inspections, closeout packages, warranty records
  • Occupied civic facilities where public services can't pause for construction
  • Long asset horizons — agencies keep buildings for decades, not lease cycles
  • Accountability to taxpayers, boards, and facility committees, not just an owner

FAQ

Government roofing questions

Do you work on prevailing wage projects?

Yes. Kodiak performs public works projects under prevailing wage requirements in both California and Nevada, with certified payroll and the compliance documentation agencies require.

Can you respond to a formal RFP or public bid?

Yes. Send us the bid documents and we'll prepare a responsive proposal — licensing, bonding, references, and scope — that fits your agency's procurement process and timeline.

Can the building stay open during the roof project?

Yes. We phase work in sections, keep everything watertight day to day, and schedule the noisiest work around public hours so counters, courtrooms, and services keep operating.

What documentation do we get at closeout?

A complete closeout package: manufacturer warranty, workmanship warranty, inspection records, and as-installed system documentation your facilities team can keep on file for the life of the roof.
