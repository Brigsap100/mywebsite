Manufacturer certifications
& awards

Certified installation is what turns a manufacturer's warranty from paper into protection. Here's why it matters — and the credentials we hold.

Why it matters

Certification is your warranty's fine print

The best membrane on the market is only as good as its installation — and manufacturers know it. That's why the strongest commercial roof warranties come with a condition attached: the installer must be certified.

01

Warranty validity

Most manufacturer system warranties — the long-term, no-dollar-limit kind owners actually want — are only issued when a contractor certified by that manufacturer performs the installation. An uncertified install can leave you with a materials-only warranty, or none at all.

02

Spec compliance

Certification means our crews are trained on the manufacturer's exact details — seam welding parameters, fastening patterns, flashing terminations — and that installations can be inspected by the manufacturer before a warranty is issued. Your roof gets built to the spec, not near it.

03

Accountability

A certified contractor answers to the manufacturer as well as to you. Certifications can be revoked over poor workmanship, so keeping them is an ongoing test — one more reason the work gets done right the first time.
