Hospitality Roofing

Roofing for hotels, resorts, and casinos across California & Nevada — including the Tahoe and Reno markets — done quietly, cleanly, and around your guests.

The challenge

The specific demands of hospitality roofs

On a hotel, resort, or casino, the roof is part of the guest experience. Noise travels into rooms, staging areas sit next to arrival drives, and the property never closes. Add California and Nevada's climate extremes — Sierra snow loads to desert heat — and hospitality roofing demands a contractor who plans around people, not just weather.

  • Guest experience first — noise, odors, and visible work must be tightly controlled
  • 24/7 occupancy: no off-hours shutdown window like an office building
  • Aesthetics matter — rooflines, pool decks, and terraces are visible to guests
  • Weather extremes from Sierra snow country around Tahoe to desert heat in Nevada
  • Amenity-heavy roofs: pools, terraces, kitchen exhaust, and dense HVAC
  • Peak seasons and event calendars that dictate when work can happen

Roof systems

Systems suited to hotels, resorts & casinos

TPO Single-Ply

Reflective membrane that cuts rooftop heat gain — a strong fit for Nevada's desert sun over conditioned guest floors and casino spaces.

EPDM

Flexible rubber membrane that shrugs off freeze-thaw cycling, well proven in Sierra and Tahoe-area snow country.

Built-Up Roofing (BUR)

Multi-ply protection for amenity roofs with heavy foot traffic, kitchen exhaust, and dense mechanical equipment.

Standing-Seam Metal

An architectural, long-life option for visible rooflines, porte-cochères, and mountain-lodge aesthetics that also sheds snow well.

FAQ

Hospitality roofing questions

Can you work while the hotel stays fully open?

Yes. We phase the roof in sections, schedule the loudest work around occupancy and events, and coordinate daily with your engineering team so guests are never surprised.

How do you keep noise away from guest rooms?

We map work zones against the occupied floors below, sequence tear-off and fastening for the least sensitive times your team identifies, and use staging that keeps equipment away from rooms in service.

Do you handle Tahoe-area snow-country roofs?

Yes. Kodiak works throughout California and Nevada, including the Tahoe and Reno markets, and recommends systems and details built for heavy snow, ice, and freeze-thaw exposure.

What happens if a leak hits during peak season?

Call 916.253.1900. Our 24/7 emergency crews respond across CA & NV to contain the leak, protect finishes and guest areas, and get a permanent repair scheduled.
