Core values

What 35+ years on commercial roofs taught us

Kodiak was founded in 1992 by people who worked the roof before they ran the company. The values below aren't a branding exercise — they're the habits that kept clients calling back for over 35 years across California and Nevada.

01

Do What We Say

If we commit to a scope, a date, or a price, we hold it. When something changes on a project — weather, hidden deck damage, supply delays — you hear it from us first, with a plan, not an excuse.

02

Safety Before Schedule

No deadline is worth a crew member's health. If conditions aren't safe, work stops until they are. A company that cuts corners on its own people will cut them on your roof too.

03

Craftsmanship That Lasts

A roof is judged in year fifteen, not week one. We detail every seam, flashing, and termination like we'll be the ones answering for it — because we usually are.

04

Straight Answers

If a repair will carry you five more years, we say so — even when a replacement would be the bigger contract. Facilities managers keep working with us because our recommendations hold up.

05

Crew First, Customer Always

We invest in the goals and careers of our employees, because well-trained, well-treated crews do better work. Taking care of our people is how we take care of your building.

06

Leave It Better

Clean staging, protected landscaping, a jobsite you don't have to think about — and a roof, when we're done, in better condition than any report promised.
