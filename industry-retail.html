The challenge

The specific demands of retail roofs

A shopping center roof isn't just a roof — it's the ceiling over dozens of businesses that can't afford to close. Retail properties bring pressures most contractors underestimate.

01

Tenant disruption = lost sales

Every hour a store closes, blocks its entrance, or loses foot traffic to construction noise costs your tenants revenue — and costs you goodwill at renewal time.

02

Customer-facing safety

Shoppers, strollers, and delivery traffic move under and around the work zone all day. Overhead protection, barricading, and clean staging are non-negotiable.

03

Anchor & inline coordination

Anchor tenants, inline shops, restaurants, and pad buildings each have different hours, lease terms, and rooftop equipment. One schedule doesn't fit all.

04

Storefront water intrusion

A slow leak over a sales floor damages inventory, drives tenant complaints, and can trigger lease disputes. Leaks at storefront transitions and canopies need fast, documented response.

05

Dense rooftop equipment

HVAC units, exhaust fans, grease vents, and signage penetrations crowd retail roofs — every one a potential leak point that must be flashed and detailed correctly.

06

Common-area maintenance budgets

Roof costs flow through CAM reconciliations, so owners need clear scopes, defensible documentation, and repair-versus-replace guidance they can share with tenants.

Systems

Roof systems we recommend for retail

The right system depends on your deck, your equipment load, and how long you plan to hold the asset. We install and maintain them all.

TPO single-ply

The workhorse of modern retail. Reflective white membrane lowers cooling loads over conditioned sales floors, installs quickly section by section, and handles dense HVAC penetrations well.

EPDM single-ply

Proven, flexible rubber membrane with a long track record on large low-slope retail roofs. A durable, economical option where reflectivity is less critical.

Built-up roofing (BUR)

Multi-ply redundancy for heavy-foot-traffic roofs — a strong fit where maintenance techs service rooftop units over occupied stores year-round.

Coatings & restoration

For occupied centers, a fluid-applied restoration can renew a serviceable roof with no tear-off, minimal noise and odor, and no disruption to stores below — often the smartest first move for a stabilized asset.

Questions

Retail roofing FAQs

Can you re-roof without closing stores?

Yes. We phase work by roof section, schedule loud or odor-producing tasks for off-hours, and keep entrances, walkways, and parking clear and protected — so tenants trade normally while the roof goes on above them.

How do you handle a leak over a tenant's sales floor?

Call our 24/7 line and a crew responds around the clock across CA & NV. We stop the water first, protect inventory and finishes, then document the cause and permanent fix in writing for your records and any lease discussions.

Do you provide documentation for CAM and capital planning?

Yes. Condition assessments, photo reports, repair histories, and repair-versus-replace recommendations — organized by building and roof section so you can budget accurately and support CAM reconciliations.

What about all the rooftop HVAC and signage penetrations?

Penetrations are where retail roofs fail. We inspect, flash, and detail every curb, vent, and sign support to manufacturer specification, and coordinate with your HVAC and sign vendors so no trade damages another's work.
