How landmark roofs get done

A closer look at the challenges behind four of our signature projects — and how over 35 years of experience shaped the approach on each one.

Case study — Mountain View, CA

Google Bay View Campus

Challenge. A flagship technology campus is one of the most scrutinized construction environments in the country. Every trade works under tight coordination, exacting design standards, and a schedule with no room for rework. Roofing on a project of this profile has to be right the first time.

Approach. Kodiak brought a deep bench of experienced foremen and a disciplined quality-control process to the work — careful sequencing with other trades, daily coordination with the general contractor, and craftsmanship at every seam and flashing.

Outcome. A watertight result on one of the West's most visible technology campuses, delivered to the standard a client like this demands.

Case study — Sacramento, CA

Golden 1 Center

Challenge. An arena in the heart of downtown Sacramento — home of the Sacramento Kings — lives on its event calendar. Roofing work has to happen around games, concerts, and public gatherings, where a single leak or a missed window can disrupt thousands of guests.

Approach. We planned the work around the venue's live schedule, protecting occupied and finished areas below, keeping staging tight in a dense urban site, and maintaining watertight conditions at every stopping point.

Outcome. Roofing systems the venue can rely on, installed without compromising the fan experience or the building's operations.

Case study — Carson City, NV

Carson Tahoe Hospital

Challenge. A critical-care medical facility never closes. Patients, sensitive equipment, and sterile environments sit directly beneath the roof, so noise, odors, dust, and any risk of water intrusion have to be managed with extreme care while the hospital keeps operating.

Approach. Kodiak coordinated closely with facilities staff to phase the waterproofing work around hospital operations — controlling access, protecting rooftop equipment, and leaving the roof watertight at the end of every shift.

Outcome. Reliable waterproofing for a facility where failure is not an option — and a facilities team that continues to trust Kodiak with its buildings.

Case study — Vacaville, CA

Midway Commerce Center

Challenge. Large industrial complexes put a premium on scale and continuity: expansive roof areas, active tenants and logistics operations below, and an owner who needs the work done efficiently without interrupting business.

Approach. Our crews delivered metal roofing and panel work with the production discipline a large industrial footprint demands — consistent detailing across long runs, careful material handling, and communication with tenants throughout.

Outcome. A durable metal roofing system across a major industrial complex, completed while the property stayed in business.
