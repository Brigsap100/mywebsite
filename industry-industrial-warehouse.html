The challenge

The specific demands of industrial roofs

Industrial and warehouse roofs are working surfaces, not just weather barriers. They carry HVAC units, exhaust fans, conveyors, and solar arrays; they absorb vibration from the equipment below; and a single leak over inventory or a production line costs far more than the repair itself.

  • Rooftop equipment, penetrations, and vibration that stress seams and flashings
  • Frequent roof traffic from maintenance trades wearing walk paths into the membrane
  • Chemical exhaust, grease, and process heat that degrade standard membranes
  • Vast low-slope areas where ponding and drainage problems compound quickly
  • Operational continuity — production and shipping can't pause for roof work
  • Inventory and equipment below that make every leak a business risk

Roof systems

Systems suited to industrial buildings

TPO Single-Ply

Reflective, heat-welded membrane that handles large open roof areas efficiently and helps manage cooling loads on conditioned warehouse space.

EPDM

Durable rubber membrane with a long track record on industrial roofs, tolerant of thermal movement across big roof planes.

Built-Up Roofing (BUR)

Multi-ply redundancy for roofs with heavy foot traffic, dense equipment, or harsh exposure — a rugged choice where the roof gets used hard.

Standing-Seam Metal

Long-life metal roofing for manufacturing and warehouse structures where slope, spans, or owner preference call for a metal system.

FAQ

Industrial roofing questions

Can you re-roof while our facility stays in operation?

Yes. We phase the work in sections, keep every completed area watertight before moving on, and coordinate staging and crane picks around your docks, truck routes, and shifts.

How do you handle all the rooftop equipment?

Every unit, curb, and penetration is detailed individually — raised, re-flashed, or re-curbed as needed — and we coordinate with your mechanical contractor when equipment has to be lifted or disconnected.

What about protecting inventory and equipment below?

Tear-off is limited to what can be made watertight the same day, interior protection is placed where work happens over sensitive areas, and we monitor weather daily before opening any roof section.

Which membrane is right for a warehouse roof?

It depends on roof traffic, exhaust and heat exposure, drainage, and budget. We assess the roof and building use first, then recommend TPO, EPDM, BUR, or metal — with the reasoning documented.
