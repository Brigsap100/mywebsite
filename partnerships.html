Manufacturer
partnerships

The relationships behind the roof. Strong manufacturer partnerships mean better systems, stronger warranties, and one team accountable for both.

Why it matters

A roof is a system — and a relationship

Your roof pairs a manufacturer's materials with a contractor's installation for decades. When those two sides work as partners rather than strangers, the owner is the one who benefits.

01

Early access to specs

Partner contractors get manufacturer detail updates, new system releases, and technical bulletins first — so the roof we design for you reflects current specifications, not last year's catalog.

02

Warranty backing

Long-term system warranties are issued on roofs installed by contractors the manufacturer knows and trusts. Established partnerships mean smoother warranty issuance — and a manufacturer more responsive if a claim ever arises.

03

Single-source accountability

When the installer and the manufacturer stand behind the same roof, there's no finger-pointing between them. One call to Kodiak, and both sides of your warranty are working the problem.

04

Direct technical support

Complex details — unusual penetrations, mixed systems, tie-ins to existing roofs — get resolved with manufacturer technical staff before installation, not improvised on the roof.

05

Material supply priority

When materials run tight across the industry, established partners get allocation priority — which protects your project schedule in ways a spot-market buyer can't match.

06

The right system, not the only system

Because we hold relationships across multiple manufacturers, we can recommend the system that fits your building and budget — instead of the one brand we happen to carry.
