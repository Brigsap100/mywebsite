The stakes

The specific demands of school & campus roofs

School roofing is as much about the calendar and the community as it is about the membrane. Districts and campuses have to keep students safe, keep classrooms dry, and make every facilities dollar answer to a board and a budget cycle.

  • Occupied-campus scheduling — the real work window is often a summer break, and it doesn't move
  • Safety around students and staff: fenced work zones, controlled deliveries, screened crews, and no shortcuts
  • District budget cycles, bond programs, and modernization funding that demand predictable scope and cost
  • Aging portfolios where classrooms, gyms, cafeterias, and portables all age — and leak — differently
  • Public accountability: school roofing is community money, spent in public view
  • Leaks that close classrooms — a stained ceiling tile in a school is a parent phone call, not just a work order

Why it matters

Miss the summer window and a re-roof waits a year — while the leaks don't. School roofing has to be planned early, staffed fully, and finished on time, because the first day of school is a deadline nothing moves.

Systems

Roof systems we recommend for schools

Most campuses mix building types and roof ages. We recommend by building — not one system across the whole district.

TPO Single-Ply

Reflective, heat-welded membrane that helps manage classroom cooling loads — a workhorse for low-slope classroom wings and multipurpose buildings.

Built-Up (BUR)

Multi-ply protection with a long track record on school buildings, well suited to roofs with regular maintenance foot traffic.

EPDM

Durable synthetic rubber membrane with long service life — a dependable option for districts planning decades ahead.

Standing-Seam Metal

Concealed-fastener metal for gyms, entries, and sloped roof areas — low maintenance across many school budget cycles.

FAQ

School roofing questions

Can a re-roof really be finished within a single summer break?

That's how we plan school work. The key is starting early: assessment, scope, and material orders happen during the school year, so crews mobilize the day campus empties. If a roof genuinely can't fit one window, we phase it by building and tell you that up front — not in August.

What if work has to happen while students are on campus?

We work on occupied campuses under a documented safety plan agreed with your facilities team: fenced and secured work zones, deliveries and crane picks scheduled outside student hours, and screened Kodiak crews who understand they're guests on a school site.

How can you help us plan for a bond or modernization program?

With roof condition assessments across your sites. A clear picture of which roofs need replacement, which can be extended with restoration, and which just need maintenance lets you phase work across budget years and support funding requests with documentation.

A classroom is leaking right now — what do we do?

Call 916.253.1900. Our emergency crews respond 24/7 across California and Nevada to stop active leaks and secure the roof, so classrooms reopen quickly and the permanent repair can be planned properly.
