The stakes

The specific demands of arena & venue roofs

Stadiums, arenas, theaters, and event centers combine some of the most complex roof geometry in commercial construction with an audience of thousands sitting directly underneath. The margin for error — in waterproofing, in safety, in scheduling — is unlike any other building type.

  • Long-span and architectural roof structures with complex geometry, transitions, and drainage
  • Event calendars that dictate when work can happen — and when everything must stop and be secured
  • Spectator and public safety: strict overhead protection, tie-off, and debris control above occupied space
  • High-visibility roofs that are part of the venue's architecture and brand — the finish has to look as good as it performs
  • Concentrated rooftop rigging, catwalks, broadcast equipment, and penetrations that all need clean waterproofing details
  • Leaks that can't wait: water over a court, stage, or seating bowl threatens events, not just finishes

Why it matters

A venue roof fails in public. Postponed events, wet seats, and a drip over center court all happen in front of an audience — so venue roofing has to be planned around the calendar and executed without a visible misstep.

Systems

Roof systems we recommend for venues

Venue roofs often combine several systems on one building — architectural metal where the roof is on display, membrane systems over the back-of-house.

Standing-Seam Metal

Interlocking, concealed-fastener panels for long-span and architectural roof areas — the clean lines and longevity signature venues demand.

TPO Single-Ply

Heat-welded reflective membrane for large low-slope areas over concourses, back-of-house, and support buildings.

EPDM

Flexible synthetic rubber membrane that handles the movement and temperature swings of large roof structures.

Built-Up (BUR)

Multi-ply redundancy for roof areas with heavy foot traffic from rigging, broadcast, and maintenance crews.

FAQ

Venue roofing questions

Can roofing work happen while our venue keeps hosting events?

Yes — that's how most venue projects run. We phase the work between events, secure and weatherproof every open area before each event, and coordinate daily with venue operations so roofing never appears on your list of event-day risks.

How do you keep spectators and staff safe during roof work?

With a written plan before work starts: overhead protection and debris containment above occupied areas, controlled material handling, full fall-protection compliance, and clear communication about where and when crews are working.

Our roof is part of the building's architecture. Can you match the original design?

Architectural metal and specialty roofing are core Kodiak services. We work from the original details — or help develop new ones — so repairs and replacements blend with the design instead of standing out from it.

What if a leak appears right before an event?

Call 916.253.1900. Our emergency crews respond 24/7 across California and Nevada, with the immediate goal of containing water away from seating, staging, and playing surfaces so the event can go on.
