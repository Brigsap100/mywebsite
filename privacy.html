Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us through this website.

Effective date: [PLACEHOLDER — confirm before publishing]

Kodiak Roofing & Waterproofing ("Kodiak," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information this website collects, how we use it, and how to reach us with questions.

Information we collect

This website does not offer user accounts and does not require you to register to browse it. The only personal information we collect is what you choose to submit through our forms:

  • Contact and quote requests — your name, company, email, phone number, and the message you write.
  • Service requests — your name, company, contact details, building address, and a description of the problem.
  • Job applications — your name, contact details, the position you're applying for, and your application answers.

Form submissions are sent to Kodiak's internal intake system so our team can follow up with you.

How we use your information

We use the information you submit only to:

  • Respond to your inquiries, quote requests, and service requests
  • Dispatch crews and coordinate work you've asked us to do
  • Review and respond to employment applications

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your information is used by Kodiak to serve you — nothing else.

Third-party services

Like most websites, this site relies on a small number of third-party services to operate:

  • Website hosting — this site is served by a third-party hosting provider, which may log standard technical data (such as IP addresses) as part of normal operation.
  • Google Fonts — pages load typefaces from Google Fonts; your browser requests these files directly from Google's servers.

These providers operate under their own privacy policies.

Questions about privacy

If you have questions about this policy or the information you've submitted to us, call us at 916.253.1900.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
